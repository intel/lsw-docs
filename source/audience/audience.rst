Intended Audience
=================

The target audience for this publication includes Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) vendors, Solution Integrators (SIs), Operating System Vendors (OSVs), Container-as-a-Service (CaaS) providers, along with Independent Software Vendors (ISVs) that contribute to the deployment and development of solutions in the space of latency sensitive workloads, for example RAN applications. The first iteration of this publication will be as a standalone document to be made available under NDA from `Intel Resource and Documentation Center <https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/resources-documentation/developer.html>`_ (RDC) for customers and providers within the area of Telecommunications (Telco) supporting such latency sensitive workloads.
